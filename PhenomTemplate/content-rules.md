# Content rules: concise titles, scannable bullets

Limits are derived from the Brand Book's own slides unless marked *(recommended)*. If content breaks a limit, mark it **Needs edit** in the preflight table and propose a shortened version; do not truncate silently.

## Titles

| Rule | Limit | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Length | ≤ 3 words, ≤ 20 characters preferred | Deck titles: `Typography`, `Color`, `Logo`, `Imagery`, `Tone of Voice`, `Visual Style`, `Questions?` |
| Lines | ≤ 2 | Title box is 228 pt wide at 28 pt |
| Case | Sentence case, no ending period | |
| Content | Name the topic, not the message | |
| Kicker | 1–2 words, ALL CAPS, 8 pt | `MESSAGING`, `VISUAL IDENTITY`, `ART DIRECTION` |

Longer ideas go in the lead line, not the title. The lead pattern in the deck is one short headline sentence (`Color brings our brand to life.`) followed by 1–2 supporting sentences.

## Bullets and blocks

*(recommended, extrapolated from the deck's grid cells)*

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| Bullets per slide | 3–5 |
| Words per bullet | ≤ 12, one idea each |
| Lead-in | 1–3 word bold label, then one sentence (`Clear subject`, `Bold color`) |
| Grid cell | Heading + ≤ 20 words |
| Row in a 3-row layout | Heading + 1 sentence |
| Paragraph | ≤ 4 sentences |
| Sentences | Short and active; imperatives and fragments are fine |

Format:

```
Clear subject
Keep one recognizable subject at the center of each image.
```

For tables, cells hold one sentence or one quoted example. No nested bullets.

## Voice (Brand Book slides 5–7)

- Direct & personal: short confident sentences addressed to "you".
- Human-first: AI serves people; show what they get back.
- Bold & imaginative: one anchor metaphor per section; do not stack metaphors.
- Proven: back every bold claim with a number, customer or scenario, close to the claim.
- Lead with help, not features: reader's problem or goal first, capability second.
- An occasional wink is fine; keep it useful.

Example from the deck:

- Feature-first: "Phenom's self-scheduling tool syncs with interviewer calendars."
- Help-first: "Eliminate the endless email chains. Let your candidates seamlessly self-schedule and automatically sync calendars."

## Do not

- Write paragraphs in ALL CAPS.
- Put long copy over gradients or art.
- Add emoji, clip-art icons, or stock office photos.
- Mix in Poppins or PDS product-UI colors.
- Use Mirage Dust for text under 14 pt bold (fails AA on Bone).
- Rename Phenom terms already in the deck (Applied AI, Cosmic Indigo, Mirage Dust, Holi).
