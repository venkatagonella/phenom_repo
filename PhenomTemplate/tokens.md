Verified against slides 9–13 of the live deck. Matches `phenom-brand-2026` and the PPTX.

## Typography

| Role | Face | Where in the deck |
|---|---|---|
| Titles, section numerals | **Fraunces** (Regular / Medium / SemiBold) | Titles 27–28 pt; cover 40 pt; dividers 90 pt numeral + 22 pt name |
| Body, labels, tables | **DM Sans** (Light / Regular / Medium / SemiBold) | Kicker 8 pt bold caps; body 9 pt; subheads 12–13 pt bold; disclaimer 7.5 pt |
| Button labels only | **VT323** | CTA buttons (slide 13) |

Rules from slide 10:

- Sentence case as the standard.
- ALL CAPS sparingly: titles, short headings, subheadings; never paragraphs.
- SemiBold only where hierarchy needs it.
- White or black text depending on background.
- Brand color only for key words or moments.

### Scale (pt, 720 × 405 canvas)

| Use | Size |

The deck is a reading document, so body is small. For slides that will be projected, keep the geometry and style but raise body to ≥ 12 pt and trim copy to fit. This is a skill recommendation, not a deck rule; say so when you do it.

### Contrast caveat

Mirage Dust `#AD7B8B` on Bone is 3.08:1. The source deck uses it for 13 pt bold row labels, which fails AA (4.5:1). Use Mirage Dust for numerals and text ≥ 18 pt (or 14 pt bold). For smaller text use black, Charcoal `#414240` or Cosmic Indigo `#373779`.
