3. Recruitment Lead

Portfolio Capacity Aging

Job statement When my team owns many open jobs, I want capacity, aging, and process aligned across cells, so hiring stays on track without me running one requisition end-to-end like a recruiter.

Core JTBD

Compete synthesis · Phenom Analytics Recruiter Manager + CRM portfolio

# JTBD Entities Phenom surfaces Must not
L1 See the full slate of open jobs for my team / org slice Job portfolio Jobs list / detail; Spotlights; Recruiter Dashboard (Insights) Day-to-day Inbox / Campaigns as primary work
L2 Assign recruiters and rebalance capacity Job, hiring team Jobs assign (needs CRM Recruiter); workload / ownership clarity Sourcer-only license for assign
L3 Spot slow or stuck jobs (aging / funnel risk) Job, application, report Analytics RX (Hiring Funnel, Jobs Report, Time to Hire); metric → job drill-down Personal outreach ownership
L4 Unblock stuck reqs and handoffs Job, opening, hiring team Ownership change; team unblock playbooks (not personal schedule/SMS ops) Day-to-day publish / offer on every req
L5 Align SLAs and process across jobs Job template, stage plan Process standards; Automations Admin (stack) if they own recipes Master / Feature / Traffic Config
L6 Report hiring results to HR and the business Openings filled, offers, closed jobs Recruiter Profiles; leadership brief / export; Report Builder (separate roles) Running one req like Primary Recruiter
