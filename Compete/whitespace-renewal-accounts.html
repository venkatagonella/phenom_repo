Renewal shortlist by region and industry

NA Top 50 and Intl Top 20 accounts from Whitespace data · Sheet2 with a dated renewal band and Swap of at least $100,000. Challenger list accounts are excluded. Built 24 Aug 2026 from the 23 Aug 2026 sheet export (701 source rows).

1. Approach

The list is not “every customer.” It is the subset that is both commercially large enough and close enough to renewal to act on.

  1. 1

    Start from account-level rows

    Use Sheet2 (701 named accounts). Sheet1 is industry averages and is not used here.

  2. 2

    Read the fields by value, not by the shifted header names

    Headers from column B onward sit one column to the right of the data. Region is North America / INTL / India / RoW (values sit in the column labeled Tier). Industry is Healthcare, Retail, and so on (values sit in the column labeled Region). Swap is the numeric ACV amount in the column labeled Swap. Renewal band is 0–3mo … 25mo+ (values sit in the column labeled Renewal).

  3. 3

    Keep only material Swap

    Drop Swap = 0 and any Swap under $100,000. Also drop non-numeric Swap values.

  4. 4

    Keep only dated renewal bands

    Keep 0–3mo, 4–9mo, 10–12mo, 13–24mo, and 25mo+. Drop Absent and Past due so the list is a forward renewal book.

  5. 5

    Drop the Challenger list

    Keep only NA Top 50 and Intl Top 20. Challenger accounts are out of scope for this shortlist.

  6. 6

    Segment Region → Industry → renewal band

    Within each industry, accounts are ordered soonest band first, then highest Swap.
