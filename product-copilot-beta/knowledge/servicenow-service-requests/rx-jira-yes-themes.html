CRM Core 334 SRs · 28.7% · Jira Yes 155 / No 179 · 6 themes

Across Jira Yes and No Service Requests, Core pain is status moves that will not stick, jobs that will not close or post correctly, ATS/LinkedIn/forwarding breaks, and access that disappears after email or role changes. Recruiters cannot self-serve these — they open tickets.

Hiring status / pipeline / stages / lead status

97 8.3% of CRM-area SRs
Jira Yes 50 · Jira No 47 (Service Requests with / without a Jira update)

Problem Recruiters cannot change lead or hiring status (dropdown empty, “no further transitions”), pipeline does not update after HM forward, and allowed moves differ for similar candidates — on both Jira Yes and No tickets.

What to do in Phenom 97 SRs
  • Show which status moves are allowed for each customer — stop empty dropdowns and “no further transitions.” 97 SRs
  • When status should update after HM forward or automation, show it immediately or show why it did not. 97 SRs
  • If an automation changed status, show that on the candidate. 97 SRs
  • Let recruiters clear stuck leads without calling Support. 97 SRs

  • Make close / publish / unpublish work for the assigned recruiter and say why the action is blocked. 77 SRs

CRM Profile 260 SRs · 22.3% · Jira Yes 145 / No 115 · 2 themes
