## Before

> **Unlocking Seamless Scheduling: Introducing Smart Reschedule** 🚀
>
> In today's fast-paced hiring landscape, every second counts. That's why we're thrilled to introduce Smart Reschedule — a game-changing feature that empowers recruiters to effortlessly manage interview changes. It's not just a feature; it's a whole new way of working.
>
> - **Effortless:** Candidates can reschedule in just a few clicks.
> - **Intelligent:** Availability is checked automatically.
> - **Seamless:** Everyone stays in sync.
>
> The result? Happier candidates, happier recruiters, and a smoother hiring journey for all.

## After

> **Candidates can now reschedule interviews themselves**
>
> From 26.11, a candidate who can't make an interview can pick a new slot from the link in their confirmation email. Phenom offers only times when every interviewer is free, updates the calendar invites, and tells the recruiter what changed.
>
> Recruiters no longer have to swap emails to move an interview. Self-reschedule is on by default; you can turn it off per interview template.

## What changed

- Dropped the headline hype, the emoji and "it's not just X, it's Y".
- Replaced the three one-word bullets with what actually happens, in order.
- Added the facts a reader needs: release, who acts, where to turn it off.
