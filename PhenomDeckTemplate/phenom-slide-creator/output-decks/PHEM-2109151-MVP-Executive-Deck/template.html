The Business Challenge
Every recruiter on a job looks the same to the system — and it's costing teams time
Enterprise talent teams staff a job with several kinds of recruiters — a lead recruiter, supporting recruiters, sourcers, and coordinators. Today the platform collapses them all into one "Recruiter" role, so notifications, workspace views, and ATS sync can't tell them apart.
The problem today
Notification fatigue & duplicates — everyone gets every alert, and a person holding two roles on a job is notified twice.
Undifferentiated "My Jobs" — jobs where a user only supports clutter the same view as the jobs they actually lead.
Lost ATS fidelity — the ATS already distinguishes sourcers and secondary recruiters, but the platform flattens them into "Recruiter."
What the business is asking for
Recognise distinct recruiter-family roles on the hiring team.
Route notifications by role and send exactly one alert per event.
Focus "My Jobs" and filters on the role that matters to each user.
Preserve the ATS's granular roles when a job is pulled in.
1
Frame the pain in the customer's language: one flat "Recruiter" role drives notification noise, cluttered work views, and loss of ATS role fidelity. The ask is role-awareness across notifications, filters, and integration.

New recruiter-family roles
Phase 1 adds recruiter-family roles, each switched on per tenant by admin configuration. Sourcer and Recruiting Coordinator become new top-level roles; Secondary Recruiter sits under Recruiter.
Leading
Primary Recruiter
Today's Recruiter. Owns the job — "My Jobs" keeps showing only where the user is Primary Recruiter.
Supporting
Secondary Recruiter
A child role under Recruiter for additional recruiters on the same requisition.
Supporting
Sourcer
New top-level role for sourcing specialists, with its own job spotlight.
Supporting
Recruiting Coordinator

2
Sourcer and Recruiting Coordinator are new parent-level roles; Secondary Recruiter is modelled as a child of Recruiter. All are config-gated per tenant, and each gets its own spotlight.
MVP · Configuration & CRM
Admin-controlled, role-aware CRM
Configuration & assignment
Each role is enabled or disabled per tenant by an admin — separately for Secondary Recruiter, Sourcer and Coordinator.
Roles can be associated to a job before it is published.
Only the Primary Recruiter adds users to these roles; assignments stay editable after publish.
Views, spotlights & ATS
Each enabled role gets its own spotlight — "Sourcer Jobs", "Secondary Recruiter Jobs", "Coordinator Jobs"; "My Jobs" still lists Recruiter jobs.
Existing filters and advanced filters work as-is for the new roles.
Roles configured in the ATS create placeholders in CRM — filled manually by admins / Primary Recruiter in MVP.
Guardrail: once a job has a user assigned to one of these roles, that role's setting can no longer be turned off.
3
Admins gate each role per tenant; only Primary Recruiter assigns users. Role spotlights and existing filters carry the experience. ATS-configured roles create placeholders filled manually in MVP, and the setting locks once a role is in use.
MVP · Notifications
One person, one notification
Exactly one email
Even when a user holds multiple roles on the same job, only one email notification is sent per event.
Last-held-role context
Notifications reflect the most recent role — a Recruiter later made Sourcer receives Sourcer notifications, not Recruiter.
Inherited settings
Notification preferences are inherited directly from recruiter-level settings.
Not in MVP: SMS / WhatsApp delivery and per-role personalization.
4
The headline outcome: de-duplicated notifications. Context follows the last-held role, and settings inherit from the recruiter level. SMS/WhatsApp and per-role personalization are explicitly out of MVP.
MVP · Reports & Analytics
Visibility into every role's contribution
What's planned
Reporting is enabled per the roles a user holds on jobs, so their activity can be analysed by role.
Primary Recruiter reporting capabilities are cloned for Secondary Recruiter, Sourcer and Coordinator.
In practice
A user who is both Secondary Recruiter and Sourcer can be reported on separately for each role.
Leaders gain a clear view of each role's contribution to a requisition.
5
Reporting becomes role-aware: activity is analysable by the role a user held, with Primary Recruiter reporting capabilities cloned to the new roles so a multi-role user can be measured per role.
