Support Recruiter role

A Support Recruiter steps in for the Main Recruiter on short notice, or works the same job with them. They can do the same work on that job and its candidates. Their home screen and their messages stay their own.

In short. Same access to the job and the candidates. Different default view. Different messages. Jobs they only support stay off their home screen until they open the support tab or turn on a filter.

Roles

Phenom creates one new role. The other three already exist. They have to stay separate, or support jobs will show up in the wrong lists.

New role

Support Recruiter

Added on a job to cover for the Main Recruiter, or to work that job with them.

  • Can do the same tasks on that job and its candidates.
  • Chooses their own messages for those jobs.
  • Those jobs stay off My Jobs, My open jobs, campaigns, leads, and the default meeting list until they ask to see them.
Already exists

Main Recruiter

The recruiter who owns the job. Today this is the person assigned to the job. Phenom needs to store it as its own role name.

  • Home screen, My open jobs, and meetings they set up stay tied to this role.
  • Their message settings stay separate from their support-job settings.
Already exists

Interviewer

Someone on a meeting. This is not a job role. Sitting on an interview does not make them a Support Recruiter.

  • They still see meetings where they are the interviewer.
  • That job does not land in My open jobs or in their support jobs.
Already exists

Hiring Manager

Sends screening feedback. They stay a Hiring Manager. They do not become a recruiter.

  • When they submit a screening evaluation, the Support Recruiter can choose to be told.
  • Their feedback sits on the job, where both recruiters can read it without tagging anyone.

What each role can do

Use the buttons to read one role at a time.

SR-01 Support Recruiter Hiring team

Put the role on the job

A job can have a Main Recruiter and one or more Support Recruiters. The same person can be Main Recruiter on one job and Support Recruiter on another. Interviewer and Hiring Manager stay different roles.

Jobs, search, messages, and meetings can all tell which role the person has on that job.

SR-02 Support Recruiter Job and candidate tasks

Do the same work on the jobs they support

On a job where they are Support Recruiter, they can do every task the Main Recruiter can do, for the job and its candidates. This covers a short-notice cover and working the job together.

They cannot do those tasks on jobs where they are neither role.

SR-03 Support Recruiter Notifications

Choose messages for support jobs separately

In Notifications, they get a second set of choices for jobs where they are the Support Recruiter. This sits next to the choices for their Main Recruiter jobs. Each message can be turned on for the CRM bell, for email (Outlook), or for both.

Changing support-job choices does not change main-job choices. A support job only sends the messages they turned on.

SR-04 Support Recruiter Notifications

Every message can be switched on, including screening feedback

The support list includes every message type, including ones that today only exist for the person assigned to the job. When a Hiring Manager submits a screening evaluation, that message is one of them.

They can turn this on even if they are not the job assignee. The same applies in Notifications, Following Triggers, and Scheduling Notifications.

SR-05 Support Recruiter Dashboard

The home screen starts with their own work

Overview, My Jobs, Discover new leads, My Email Campaigns, and My SMS campaigns show work where they are the Main Recruiter, or items they created. Jobs they only support are left out.

Counts such as My Open Jobs, My Leads, and My Applicants do not go up because of a support job.

SR-06 Support Recruiter Dashboard

They can choose to see a colleague’s work

They can filter Overview, My Jobs, Discover new leads, and the campaign widgets to include colleagues on the same jobs.

The filter starts off. Turning it on does not change what they see the next time they sign in.

SR-07 Support Recruiter Meetings

My meetings stays small

My meetings shows meetings they set up as Main Recruiter, and meetings where they are an Interviewer. Meetings on a job they only support stay off this list.

Those support-job meetings also stay out of My pending, My upcoming, and My completed.

SR-08 Support Recruiter Meetings

A second tab for support-job meetings

A second tab lists meetings for jobs where they are the Support Recruiter.

Pending, upcoming, and completed still work inside that tab. The two tabs stay separate.

SR-09 Support Recruiter Spotlight

Find support jobs by the Main Recruiter’s email

In Spotlight, they can pick one or more Main Recruiter email addresses. Phenom shows jobs where those people are the Main Recruiter and they are the Support Recruiter.

Clearing the filter brings back their own default list.

SR-10 Support Recruiter Jobs

Two job lists

My open jobs shows only jobs where they are the Main Recruiter. A second tab shows jobs where they are the Support Recruiter.

A job is never in both tabs for the same person. Open Jobs, All Jobs, and My Requisitions stay as they are.

SR-11 Support Recruiter Job notes

Read job notes without being tagged

On a job they support, they see the job notes, including Hiring Manager feedback and notes from the Main Recruiter. Nobody has to tag them.

A new Support Recruiter sees the notes already on the job. Those notes do not appear on the candidate profile.

MR-01 Main Recruiter Dashboard and jobs

Their home screen stays their own jobs

Overview, My Jobs, Discover new leads, both campaign widgets, and My open jobs show jobs they own or items they created. Jobs where they are only the Support Recruiter stay out.

Helping on a colleague’s job does not increase My open jobs or the home-screen counts.

MR-02 Main Recruiter Notifications

Their message settings stay separate

Choices for jobs they own do not change when they change choices for jobs they support.

Turning a support message off still sends that message for a job they own, if that switch is on.

MR-03 Main Recruiter Meetings

Meetings they set up stay in My meetings

If they booked the meeting as Main Recruiter, it stays on My meetings. Meetings where they are an Interviewer stay there too.

Adding the support meetings tab does not move these meetings.

MR-04 Main Recruiter Job notes

Share feedback on the job

Notes they write in the job notes are visible to every Main Recruiter and Support Recruiter on that job. They do not need to tag anyone. The notes stay off the candidate profile.

Someone who is not a Main or Support Recruiter on that job cannot read them.

INT-01 Interviewer Meetings

They still see their own interviews

If they are an interviewer on a meeting, that meeting shows in My meetings. This is true even when they are not the Main or Support Recruiter on the job.

They see that one meeting. They do not see the other meetings on that job.

INT-02 Interviewer Access

An interview is not a job assignment

Being an interviewer does not add the job to My open jobs or the support jobs tab. It does not turn on support messages, and it does not give them the recruiter’s tasks on that job.

They can join their interview. They cannot run the job.

HM-01 Hiring Manager Notifications

Their screening evaluation can alert the Support Recruiter

When they submit a screening evaluation, the Main Recruiter can be told, and each Support Recruiter can be told separately, in the CRM and by email.

A Support Recruiter who turned the message off does not get it, even if they can open the job.

HM-02 Hiring Manager Job notes

Their feedback stays on the job

Feedback they give is stored in the job notes. Every Main Recruiter and Support Recruiter on that job can read it. Nobody has to be tagged.

The same feedback does not show in the candidate’s general notes.

HM-03 Hiring Manager Access

They stay a Hiring Manager

Being the Hiring Manager does not make them a Main Recruiter or a Support Recruiter. Their screens stay the hiring-manager screens.

They do not get the recruiter job tabs, the recruiter home screen, or the support message settings.

Product areas to build

Eight product areas need development. Today, anyone on the hiring team can be treated as “on the job.” That is not enough. Phenom has to know who is the Main Recruiter and who is the Support Recruiter, and use that in each area below.

Product area What to develop
Access and roles A Support Recruiter role on the job, stored separately from Main Recruiter, Interviewer, and Hiring Manager.
Jobs The same job and candidate tasks for the Support Recruiter. My open jobs for main jobs only. A new tab for support jobs.
Recruiter dashboard Overview, My Jobs, and Discover new leads start with the user’s own main-recruiter work. A filter can add colleagues on the same jobs.
Campaigns My Email Campaigns and My SMS Campaigns show campaigns the user created. Support jobs do not add campaigns unless the filter is on.
Notifications A second set of CRM and email choices for support jobs, including every message type and Hiring Manager screening evaluations.
Meetings My meetings for meetings they set up and interviews they sit on. A second tab for support-job meetings. Show only candidates they are allowed to see.
Spotlight A filter to pick Main Recruiter emails and see the jobs where the user is the Support Recruiter.
Notes Notes that belong to the job. Main and Support Recruiters can read and write them, including Hiring Manager feedback, without tagging. They stay off the candidate profile.
1

Access and roles

Who is on the job

Add Support Recruiter as a role on the hiring team. Keep Main Recruiter as its own role, not just “assigned to the job.” Leave Interviewer on the meeting and Hiring Manager as they are.

  • A person can hold different roles on different jobs.
  • “My” lists use Main Recruiter. Support lists use Support Recruiter.
  • Being an Interviewer or a Hiring Manager does not fill either list.
2

Jobs

Job list and job work

On a support job, the Support Recruiter can do the same work as the Main Recruiter. The job list itself stays split.

  • My open jobs = jobs where I am the Main Recruiter.
  • New tab = jobs where I am the Support Recruiter.
  • Open Jobs, All Jobs, and My Requisitions stay as they are.
  • The My open jobs count matches the home screen.
3

Recruiter dashboard

Home screen

Overview, My Jobs, and Discover new leads open on work the user owns as Main Recruiter. Add a filter for colleagues who work the same jobs. The filter starts off.

  • Covers the Overview cards, including My Open Jobs, My Leads, and My Applicants.
  • A support-only job does not change those numbers until the filter is turned on.
4

Campaigns

Email and SMS

My Email Campaigns and My SMS Campaigns show campaigns the user created. Campaigns on a job they only support stay hidden until they use the same colleague filter as the dashboard.

5

Notifications

CRM alerts and Outlook email

Next to today’s message settings, add a second set for jobs where the user is the Support Recruiter. Each message can be on or off for the CRM and for email.

  • Cover Notifications, Following Triggers, and Scheduling Notifications.
  • Include messages that today only go to the job assignee.
  • Include “screening evaluation submitted” when a Hiring Manager sends it. Screening has to raise that event for the Support Recruiter, not only for the assignee.
  • Main-job choices and support-job choices save separately.
6

Meetings

Interview schedule

Keep My meetings for meetings the user set up as Main Recruiter, plus meetings where they are an Interviewer. Add a second tab for meetings on support jobs. Pending, upcoming, and completed stay inside each tab.

  • Do not put every meeting in one list.
  • In both tabs, show a candidate only if that person is allowed to see them.
  • Fix the open bug where meetings ignore candidate visibility. Do this before the support tab goes live.
7

Spotlight

Saved views

On customize view, add a multi-select of Main Recruiter email addresses. The result is jobs where those people are the Main Recruiter and the signed-in user is the Support Recruiter.

8

Notes

Feedback on the job

Add a notes type that belongs to the job. Main Recruiters and Support Recruiters on that job can read and write it, including Hiring Manager feedback. They do not tag each other.

  • A new Support Recruiter sees notes that are already there.
  • Someone removed from the job loses access.
  • These notes do not show on the candidate profile.

One rule for everyone

People only see what they need

Example. Alex is Main Recruiter on job A, Support Recruiter on job B, and Interviewer on one meeting for job C.

  • Job A is in My open jobs.
  • Job B is only on the support jobs tab.
  • My meetings shows meetings Alex set up, plus the one interview on job C.
  • The rest of job B’s meetings are only on the support meetings tab.
  • Job C does not become Alex’s job.
